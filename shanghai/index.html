Lunch on Anfu Road安福路午餐

Bakery lunch at the start of the lanes.

Do · Didi ~10 min from the hotel, or walk 2 km south down Wulumuqi Rd. Baker & Spice, 195 Anfu Rd (06:00–22:00, croissants ~¥35, omelette ~¥68, no queue). RAC, 322 Anfu Rd (Breton galettes ~¥70–98) queues; fine after 14:00. Sunflour, 308 Anfu Rd (08:00–21:30) is the quiet alternative. Lazy option 1 km from the hotel: Din Tai Fung, Jing'an Kerry Centre, 1515 Nanjing W Rd.

Anfu Road & Wukang Road安福路 · 武康路

Plane trees, 1920s villas, the best lanes in the city. Flat, 1–2 h at your own pace.

Do · Anfu Rd: Shanghai Dramatic Arts Centre (288), boutiques, cafés; shops change fast, treat it as a map not a list. Turn onto Wukang Rd 武康路: Ferguson Lane 武康庭 courtyard (376), then Wukang Mansion 武康大楼 at the south end, Hudec's 1924 ship-prow block, best from the opposite pavement. Optional interior: Soong Ching Ling Residence 宋庆龄故居, 1843 Huaihai M Rd, 2 min from the Mansion, Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00, intact 1920s villa and garden.

Access · Shops are Alipay/WeChat only; few public toilets; narrow pavements.

Nanjing East Road南京东路步行街

1.6 km car-free straight to the river; neon as it gets dark.

Do · Walk east from People's Square. Look at Shanghai No.1 Food Store 第一食品商店 (720 Nanjing E Rd, 1925, ground-floor snack hall — look, buy a snack, keep going), the four 1920s–30s department-store facades at the Henan Rd end (Wing On 永安, Sincere 先施), the quieter Snøhetta-paved last stretch, and the Peace Hotel 和平饭店 lobby at the river end (1929 Art Deco; walk in; Jazz Bar band from ~19:00, no cover). Jiujiang Rd, one block south, has the same architecture and a tenth of the crowd.

Access · Ignore anyone offering a "tea ceremony" or "art student exhibition"; classic scam.

The Bund at twilight外滩

The skyline across the river as the lights come on.

Do · Sunset 17:23, full dark ~17:50, Pudong lights on ~18:00–18:30, off ~22:00. Chen Yi Square 陈毅广场 opposite Nanjing E Rd is the postcard and the most crowded 50 m. Walk north to Waibaidu Bridge 外白渡桥 (1907 steel) for the quieter angle and the Rockbund lanes behind the Peninsula. The promenade is 1.5 km end to end.

Dinner: Lao Zheng Xing老正兴菜馆

The oldest Shanghainese house in the city (1862), ten minutes from the river.

Do · 556 Fuzhou Rd 福州路556号. Order 油爆虾 fried river shrimp, 红烧肉 braised pork, 八宝辣酱 eight-treasure sauce, 糟溜黄鱼 yellow croaker. ~¥150–250 pp. Multi-floor, queues clear fast; arrive before 18:00 or call 021-6322-2624. Fallback: Bund Family Banquet 外滩家宴 on Jiujiang Rd, mid-price, takes a number at peak.

Hours · typical 11:00–14:00 / 17:00–21:00 (unverified for 2026).

After dinner: ferry or terrace轮渡 · 露台

The skyline from the water for ¥2, or one drink above it.

Do · Fuxing Rd ferry 复兴东路渡口 (1 Fuxing E Rd, 900 m south of the Bund's south end) crosses to Dongchang Rd, Pudong, until 21:00, ¥2 by Alipay; ride over and straight back. Terraces: Lost Heaven on the Bund rooftop bar (4F, 17 Yan'an E Rd, no queue), Sir Elly's Terrace at the Peninsula (13F, 32 Zhongshan E 1st Rd, 17:00–24:00, cocktails ~¥130–180, the best open view), The Captain (6F, 37 Fuzhou Rd, cheapest). Skip Bar Rouge.

Riverside by bike西岸滨江骑行

Car-free riverside with its own bike lane; golden hour on the water around 17:00.

Do · Shared bikes (HelloBike 哈啰 via Alipay, ~¥1.5–2.5 per 30 min) along Longteng Ave, or Blueberry Cycling rental at West Bund Art Center. Ride south toward Lupu Bridge and back, 2–3 h with stops. The bike lane and running track run side by side; no cars anywhere on it, separation from walkers is partial.
